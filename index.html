AI Appraiser Tools

Free, open-source tools for real estate appraisers — built by The Appraiser Coach.

What this is

A growing collection of practical tools for residential appraisers, built with AI and shared openly. Use them, copy them, suggest improvements, build your own.

Nothing to install and no signup. The pages run in your browser and we never see your files.

New here? How the prompt tools work

  1. Pick a tool below and click 📋 Copy prompt on its page.
  2. Open Claude.ai or ChatGPT in another tab and start a new chat.
  3. Paste the prompt, attach your report PDF with the paperclip, and send.
  4. Review what comes back like a reviewer would. You are still the appraiser.

You'll need a paid AI plan (Claude Pro or ChatGPT Plus, about $20/month). Free plans usually can't read PDFs or photos well enough. Not sure where to start? Try the 133-Step QC Reviewer on your last report.

Appraiser Exam Prep (website, no AI needed)

Study for the national appraiser exam in your browser. 198 questions and 14 topic briefs built from the AQB Exam Content Outlines effective April 1, 2026, with separate tracks for Certified Residential, Licensed Residential, and Trainee / course exams. Learn one content area at a time, drill it until the explanations stop surprising you, then sit a timed mock exam and get a per-area breakdown of what to study next. Nothing to install and no signup — your progress is saved in your own browser and never uploaded anywhere.

Depreciated Cost Calculator

Drop in an appraisal PDF, get suggested adjustment ranges for the construction-related elements of comparison (GLA, basement, garage) derived from the report's own Cost Approach. Auto-extracts the subject cost approach and the comp features from the sales grid.

133-Step QC Reviewer (prompt template)

A pre-submission QC checklist for any 1004 / 1073 / 1004C / 1025 / 2055 / G-PAR report. Copy the prompt, paste into Claude.ai or ChatGPT, attach your appraisal PDF — get back severity-rated concerns with step references. Requires a paid Claude.ai or ChatGPT subscription.

Revision Response Writer (prompt template)

Turn any AMC / underwriter revision request into addendum-ready response text in the appraiser's voice. Drop the report PDF plus the revision request, get back numbered responses with location citations, USPAP-grounded refusals where appropriate, and placeholders flagged for review. Requires a paid Claude.ai or ChatGPT subscription.

Engagement Letter Parser (prompt template)

Drop in an engagement letter PDF, get every field you need to set up the order in TOTAL — property address, borrower, lender, AMC, fee, due date, loan type, sale price, case numbers, plus all contacts (borrower POC, lender POC, buyer's & seller's agents). Works across Mercury VMP, Aloft, ClassValuation, UWM, and any other AMC layout. Includes machine-readable JSON output for import scripts. Requires a paid Claude.ai or ChatGPT subscription.

County Records Puller (prompt template, Claude in Chrome)

Give it a subject address and Claude opens your county's own public sites in your browser — assessor, treasurer, GIS, recorder, plat — and returns one subject data sheet: owner, APN, legal description, site and improvement data, taxes, 3+ years of ownership history, and map links, with a source link for every figure and a flag list of anything missing or conflicting. Works in any county. Read-only: it stops for CAPTCHAs and logins and never buys documents. Requires the Claude in Chrome extension and a paid Claude plan.

Photo Quality Checker (prompt template, vision-required)

Drop in a completed report PDF. The AI walks every photo and flags: missing required angles (front/rear/street/comps), missing interior coverage (kitchen/baths/mechanicals — FHA & VA escalate this), condition-rating mismatches (C2 claimed but photo shows obvious wear), quality-rating consistency across the lineup, and quality issues (blurry, dark, obstructed, watermarked stock photos). Severity-rated output. Requires a paid Claude.ai or ChatGPT subscription with a vision-capable model.

Neighborhood Narrative Builder (prompt template)

Give it the subject address plus your four neighborhood-character trend boxes, get back suggested N/S/E/W major-road boundaries plus publication-ready Neighborhood Description and Market Conditions narratives. Third-person report voice, no marketing language, [PLACEHOLDER] tags wherever specific numbers are needed. Requires a paid Claude.ai or ChatGPT subscription.

 →

Reconciliation Narrative Writer (prompt template, vision-required)

Drop in a completed report PDF — or just a screenshot of the adjusted grid — and get a publication-ready reconciliation: comp-status classification (closed vs pending vs active), suggested weights summing to 100% across closed sales only, and the three-paragraph reconciliation narrative. Correctly excludes active and pending comps from weighting (the most common reviewer kickback). Requires a paid Claude.ai or ChatGPT subscription with a vision-capable model.

Comp Verification (prompt template)

Turn your comp list into professional verification emails to the listing agents — one email per agent, combining multiple comps from the same agent so nobody is contacted twice. Asks about concessions, buyer-agent compensation, multiple offers, related parties, and close price/date. Paste your comps into Claude.ai or ChatGPT, get back ready-to-send emails. Requires a paid AI subscription.

AI Voice Agent Builder (prompt template)

Stand up a phone agent that answers calls while you are on an inspection. Two prompts: one coaches you through building it on any platform (Go High Level, Synthflow, Retell, Vapi, Bland, or ElevenLabs + Twilio), one is a ready-to-edit script for the agent itself.

Employee & Contractor Onboarding Kit (runs on your computer)

Onboard a new hire with one command. You enter their name, email, start date, pay, and whether they are a W-2 employee or a 1099 contractor. They get one link that walks them through six training modules with quizzes, an agreement to e-sign, and the tax forms to upload — and the finished paperwork lands filed and organized for you. Configure one file with your office details, then run it. Nothing about your office or your hires leaves your computer.
