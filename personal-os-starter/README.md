# Personal OS Starter

A folder of plain markdown files that any AI can read and write. Point Claude, ChatGPT, or
Gemini at it and that model instantly knows who you are, how you work, and what you're
working on — without you re-explaining yourself every time you open a new chat.

**The idea in one line:** your knowledge should live with you, not inside the tool.

Every time you start a new chat, your AI starts from zero. You paste the same background
in again. You explain your business again. Then a better model comes out, and you start
over. This fixes that. The files are yours, they're plain text, and they outlive whichever
model is currently winning.

## What you get

```
brain/
  CLAUDE.md        the constitution — read first, every session. Highest-leverage file.
  me.md            who you are: background, expertise, how you like to work
  business.md      what you do: services, fees, market, team
  voice.md         how you write and speak, so drafts sound like you
  goals.md         current priorities and 90-day targets
  principles.md    the rules behind your judgment calls
  processes.md     your workflows, standards, quality checkpoints
  relationships.md the people in your world
  second-brain.md  ideas, to-dos, open loops — the daily catcher
  journal.md       dated record of what was discussed and decided
```

Ten files. Start with two.

## Start here

1. **Fill out `me.md` and `business.md`.** Don't write them by hand — open
   `INTERVIEW-PROMPTS.md` and let the AI interview you. Twenty minutes, and it's better
   than anything you'd have written from a blank page.
2. **Put the folder somewhere your AI can reach** — see `SETUP.md`.
3. **Test it.** Ask: *"Based on my files, what do you know about my business?"*
4. **Add the rest over time.** A half-filled brain beats a perfect empty one.

## Then read LESSONS.md

`LESSONS.md` is the part you won't find in a blog post — the things that go wrong once
this is actually running, and why. It'll save you an afternoon.
